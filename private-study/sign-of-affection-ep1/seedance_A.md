## 생성 A — 지하철 (15초)

```
STYLE FIRST
Real live-action film footage with real human actors, shot on a cinema camera. Real skin texture and pores, real hair strands, real wool fabric, natural window light, natural lens bokeh. NOT anime, NOT 2D, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration, NOT 3D render, NOT painted. Every frame must look like a still from a Japanese live-action romance drama. Natural, slightly muted winter color grade, the same look as a quiet snowy-night drama. Widescreen 16:9.

REFERENCES
@Image1 is a real photo sheet of THE GIRL. @Image2 is a real photo sheet of THE MAN. They are real people: copy their real faces, hair, bodies, outfits and colors exactly. Never recreate the sheet layout, swatches or any printed text or names. Each person appears only once.
- THE GIRL (@Image1): petite, 158 cm, deaf; she communicates with sign language and phone memos. Long wavy pink hair, full bangs, rosy purple eyes, cream hearing aid on her RIGHT ear. Camel coat, red and pink plaid scarf, black tights, brown loafers, pink phone. Bright and cheerful.
- THE MAN (@Image2): tall and slim, 183 cm. Silver gray hair with messy bangs, deep blue eyes, clear skin. Long olive khaki coat, brown patterned knit scarf, black trousers, silver sun ring on his RIGHT hand, a large travel backpack. Calm and easygoing.

LOCATION
A daytime Japanese commuter train in winter. Silver hand rails and straps, bench seats, a route map and station display above the door with blurry unreadable text, the city sliding past the windows, soft winter daylight. A few passengers far in the background, out of focus.

SHOT 1 — medium shot by the train door, 50mm, slow push in to her face.
She stands holding a rail, scrolling social media on her phone with a little smile. A friendly Western tourist man in his 30s with a camera bag, holding a cafe guide flyer, leans in and talks to her, pointing at the flyer. Startled, she presses her palms together in apology, touches her ear and shakes her head to show she cannot hear. He keeps talking, not understanding. Her eyes fill with panic.
Lens switch.
SHOT 2 — medium two-shot, 35mm.
THE MAN, with his big backpack, steps in beside them, asks the foreigner if he needs help, and explains the way easily, pointing at the route map. The foreigner lights up, thanks him and walks off.
Lens switch.
SHOT 3 — over his shoulder, 50mm.
She recognizes him from her university, surprised. She bows to thank him, then shows him a memo typed on her phone (blurry text). He reads it and nods with a relaxed smile.
Lens switch.
SHOT 4 — close-up on him from her eye level, 85mm, his lips clearly visible.
As the train slows, he pulls his scarf below his mouth and, facing her, speaks slowly and clearly so she can read his lips, in Japanese, calm warm voice: 「君は？降りる？降りない？」
Lens switch.
SHOT 5 — close-up on her, 85mm, slow push in.
She realizes she can read every word. Her eyes widen and shine, her cheeks turn pink, and she gives a small happy nod, holding her phone to her chest.

SOUND
From her point of view: shots 1 to 3 are almost silent, as if under water, the train only a faint low rumble; no voices are heard. In shot 4 the sound gently returns and his line is the first clear voice. No background music.

RULES
Live-action in every frame. Exactly one girl, one man and one foreigner, no twins. Her hearing aid always on her RIGHT ear. The man is clearly taller. Phone screens and the flyer show only blurry text. No subtitles, no on-screen text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 편집

1. shot 1~3은 유키 시점의 무음 구간입니다. Seedance가 목소리를 넣었으면 지우고, 아주 먹먹한 저음만 남깁니다.
2. 자막(선택): shot 2 이츠오미 "Do you need help?", shot 3 유키 메모 「一緒の大学です 国際サークルのりんちゃんと友達です」(같은 대학이에요, 국제 서클 린짱 친구예요), shot 4 "너는? 내려? 안 내려?"
3. shot 4에서 소리가 돌아오는 순간이 A의 핵심입니다. 그 직전 0.5초를 완전 무음으로 비워 두면 대비가 커집니다.
