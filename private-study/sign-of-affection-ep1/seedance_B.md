## 생성 B — 밤길 (15초)

```
STYLE FIRST
Real live-action film footage with two real human actors, shot on a cinema camera. Real skin texture and pores, real hair strands, real wool fabric, real falling snow, natural lens bokeh. NOT anime, NOT 2D, NOT cartoon, NOT illustration, NOT 3D render, NOT painted. Every frame must look like a still from a Japanese live-action romance drama. Widescreen 16:9.

REFERENCES
@Image1 is a real photo sheet of THE GIRL. @Image2 is a real photo sheet of THE MAN. They are real people: copy their real faces, hair, bodies, outfits and colors exactly. Never recreate the sheet layout, swatches or any printed text or names. Each person appears only once.
- THE GIRL (@Image1): petite, 158 cm, deaf. Long wavy pink hair, full bangs, rosy purple eyes, cream hearing aid on her RIGHT ear. Camel coat, red and pink plaid scarf, black tights, brown loafers, pink phone, pale pink umbrella. Gentle, shy and quietly warm, not bubbly; small, soft reactions.
- THE MAN (@Image2): tall and slim, 183 cm. Silver gray hair with messy bangs, deep blue eyes, clean smooth skin with no freckles and no spots. Long olive khaki coat, brown patterned knit scarf, black trousers, silver sun ring on his RIGHT hand, dark navy umbrella. Calm; rarely shows big emotions.

LOCATION
The first snowy night of winter in a quiet Japanese neighborhood: a narrow street along a brick wall, then a corner by a small park with a black metal fence, snowy shrubs and bare trees. Big soft snowflakes fall steadily in every shot; fresh snow on the ground. Streetlights with soft pink and lavender glow, deep blue night, visible breath.

SHOT 1 — side tracking along the brick wall, 35mm.
They walk side by side under their umbrellas, a little awkward. A scooter speeds past close to her; he gently pulls her to the inner side and takes the outer side himself. Walking on, his ringed right hand quietly takes hers.
Lens switch.
SHOT 2 — two-shot at the park corner, 50mm.
Time to part. Shyly, she lifts her phone up beside her face with the screen turned toward him, showing a short typed memo asking for his contact (blurry text), her eyes closed and cheeks pink. She does not push it at him. He leans down a little to read it, smiles softly and takes out his own phone.
Lens switch.
SHOT 3 — she is a few steps away along the park fence, 50mm.
She stops and types with both thumbs, nervous and hopeful, screen light on her face. Lips closed. VOICE-OVER, her soft bright inner voice, Japanese: 「世界は広いですか？」
Lens switch.
SHOT 4 — he still stands at the corner under his umbrella, watching her go, 85mm.
He reads her message, a small gentle smile, and types back. Lips closed. VOICE-OVER, his low calm warm voice, Japanese, slow: 「すげぇ広い。」 short pause, 「俺を、雪の世界に入れて。」
Lens switch.
SHOT 5 — medium close-up on her by the park fence, 50mm, snow falling softly.
She reads his reply and goes still for a moment. Then she slowly turns back toward him, the phone held to her chest, and smiles softly and beautifully, her eyes gentle and shining, cheeks pink. A small, quiet smile, not a big laugh; no jumping, no raised arms, no throwing snow.
Lens switch.
SHOT 6 — close-up on him under his umbrella, 85mm.
Seeing her smile from across the street, his calm face breaks into a helpless, happy grin he cannot hold back, eyes closing.

SOUND
The two message lines are heard only as voice-over, like reading texts in their heads; no lips move during them. Footsteps in snow, the scooter whoosh in shot 1, falling snow, a soft phone vibration before each voice-over, a soft breath as she smiles in shot 5. No background music.

RULES
Live-action in every frame. Snow falls in every shot. Exactly one girl and one man, no twins, no other people except the scooter rider. Her hearing aid always on her RIGHT ear. He has no freckles. She never throws snow. Phone screens show only blurry text. No subtitles, no on-screen text, no logos, no watermark.
```
